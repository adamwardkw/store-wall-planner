# Store Wall Planner

Your shop wall, flat on your phone. Every spot visible, gaps and all.

Live: https://adamwardkw.github.io/store-wall-planner/

## How it works

1. Text a photo of the wall to Instinct.
2. Instinct texts back a map file.
3. Open this page and tap **Import the map Instinct sent**.

Then everything happens on the wall itself:

- **Tap a colour block**, then tap an empty spot - it moves there.
- With a block selected: **Label**, **Split into singles**, **Park aside** or **Remove** in the bar at the bottom.
- **Tap an empty spot** to say what it means: **Missing product**, **Empty on purpose**, **Not sure**, or **Auto** (the app guesses from what sits around the hole).
- **+ Add products**: pull to the number of spots, tap **Make bar**, then tap an empty spot. It lands as separate singles in one colour group.
- Parked blocks wait below the wall. Tap one, then tap an empty spot to put it back.
- **Bigger spots** zooms in; swipe sideways.
- Undo and redo are top right. The dots menu has Export, Import, the wall photo, Print, and Start over from the map.

Everything saves on your device. Export a plan file any time as a backup.

Colours show which spots belong together. You choose the names.
